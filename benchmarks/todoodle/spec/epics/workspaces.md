# Authless workspaces

No accounts. A workspace is reachable only through its secret, unguessable URL. The URL is the credential: lose it and access is lost; share it and the recipient becomes a collaborator. A browser cookie remembers workspaces this browser has opened.

## Stories

| ID | Title | Status |
|----|-------|--------|
