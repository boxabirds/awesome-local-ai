# Task management

Todoist-style core: projects, Inbox, quick-add, complete/edit/delete, due dates, Today view.

## Stories

| ID | Title | Status |
|----|-------|--------|
| 5 | Capture a task into my Inbox in seconds | proposed |
| 6 | Tick off, edit, and remove tasks — with undo when I slip | proposed |
| 7 | Group related tasks into projects so work stays organised | proposed |
| 8 | Give tasks due dates and see everything due today (and overdue) in one place | proposed |
| 11 | Find any task or list in seconds by typing what I remember, with results that stay live | proposed |
