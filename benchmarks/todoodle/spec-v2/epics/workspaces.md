# Authless workspaces

There are no accounts. A workspace is reachable only through its secret, unguessable URL, and that URL is the credential:
- lose it and access is lost;
- share it and the recipient becomes a collaborator;
- replace it to cut everyone else off.

A browser cookie remembers the workspaces this browser has opened. Stories in build order:
2. Start a private workspace instantly and get a secret link.
3. Get back to my workspaces from this browser.
4. Bring others in by sharing the link, with live updates.
9. Cut off anyone who has an old link by getting a fresh one.
