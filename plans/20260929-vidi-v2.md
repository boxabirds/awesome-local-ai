# Vidi v2: rebuild every stack on the v2 spec, score it, and validate the scores

Started 29 Sep 2026. Owner: Julian Harris. Status legend: **done**, **in progress**, **next**, **open** (needs a
decision), **blocked**.

## Goal

Every stack builds the v2 spec from story 1, is scored by a held-out suite written for v2, and has its
scores validated by a person watching the recordings. The v1.3 results stay published as v1.3; results are
never mixed across versions.

## Decisions taken

| Date | Decision |
|---|---|
| 29 Sep | v2 is the spec that gets evaluated; every stack is rebuilt from story 1 (no reuse of v1.3 stories: every later story builds on earlier ones, and machine time is free). |
| 29 Sep | Specs are public; held-out suites are private. The held-out suite is what protects against contamination. |
| 29 Sep | The v2.0 held-out suite is written fresh, not edited from v1 (the v1 suite was public on 23-25 Sep). |
| 29 Sep | Three stages: **build** (the agent), **score** (the held-out suite, after the run, pinned version), **judge** (a person validates each scored verdict from its recording). |
| 29 Sep | Swift 1.5 is a different model from Swift 1.0, so it is a separate combination. |
| 29 Sep | Builds may start on the v2 spec before the v2.0 suite exists: they are re-scored afterwards. |
